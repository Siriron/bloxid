WHAT YOU CAN DO

ID 🧑‍💻

Builder Profile

Register your onchain identity. Set your display name, role, specialization, and metadata URI. Your profile lives permanently on Base.

📁 🚀

Project Registry

Log every project you build. Attach a repository hash, category, and metadata so anyone can verify your work history on-chain.

🎯 🏁

Milestones

Break down your projects into milestones. Each one is timestamped and stored permanently — a verifiable trail of progress.

📦 📋

Release History

Publish version tags and release hashes for each project. A permanent, tamper-proof changelog anyone can verify.

🤝 ✅

Collaboration Attestations

Vouch for builders you've worked with. Attest their role and contribution on-chain — the most credible proof of teamwork.

📈 ⭐

Reputation Score

A pure on-chain reputation score calculated from projects, milestones, and attestations. No manipulation possible.

HOW IT WORKS

01

Connect Your Wallet

Connect MetaMask or any EVM wallet. Make sure you're on Base Mainnet. Your wallet address is your identity.

02

Create Your Builder Profile

Set your display name, role, specialization, and a metadata URI. One transaction. Lives forever on Base.

03

Register Projects

Add your projects with name, category, repo hash, and metadata. Each project gets a permanent on-chain ID.

04

Log Milestones & Releases

Track your progress and publish version releases. Every update is timestamped and verifiable by anyone.

05

Earn Attestations & Grow Your Score

Project creators can attest collaborators. Your reputation score grows with every contribution — permanently visible to the world.
